At MDSLab, students can participate in research-oriented bachelor's and master's theses and internships, working on challenging problems through software development, simulation, experimental evaluation, and hardware prototyping.

Our activities are organized around the research areas below. The topics listed represent broad research directions rather than predefined thesis projects. Specific activities are agreed upon individually based on students' interests, technical background, and ongoing research.

  <h2>How to Apply</h2>
  <p class="area-text">Students interested in a thesis or internship should identify the research areas and topics that best match their interests and contact <a href="mailto:francesco.longo@unime.it">Prof. Francesco Longo</a>.</p>
  <p class="area-text">When making a request, please indicate your degree programme, academic background, preferred research topics, and expected graduation period.</p>
